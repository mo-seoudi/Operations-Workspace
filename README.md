# Operations Workspace

A connected workspace for planning, managing, and controlling operational work.

## Product foundation

Operations Workspace is designed for an individual operations professional, an operations team, or a multi-site organization. It connects projects, jobs, tasks, people, suppliers, contracts, compliance, budgets, documents, communications, approvals, progress, and operational history.

## Initial product areas

- Dashboard and portfolio visibility
- My Work: tasks, overdue items, waiting-on, follow-ups, approvals
- Projects, workstreams, tasks, milestones, calendar and timeline
- Suppliers and supplier progress updates
- Contracts and renewal tracking
- Compliance requirements and document expiries
- Operational budgets: CAPEX/OPEX, allocated, committed, spent, forecast
- Communications and documents linked to operational context
- Risks, issues, decisions and changes
- Reporting and project history

## Product principle

Enter information once at the point where the work happens, then reuse it across project dashboards, supplier updates, management reporting, budget reviews, compliance and history.

## Status

Early product prototype.
The first build will focus on the core operations experience before adding production backend infrastructure and integrations.
